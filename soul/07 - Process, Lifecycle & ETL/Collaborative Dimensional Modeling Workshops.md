# Collaborative Dimensional Modeling Workshops

Dimensional models should be designed **collaboratively**, in facilitated **workshops** that bring **business users** together with the data-modeling team (and source-system/IT experts) — not drafted in isolation by a modeler.

## Why
- The model must reflect **how the business thinks and speaks** about its processes; users are the authority on attributes, labels, and hierarchies.
- Joint design surfaces **data realities** (what the source can actually supply) against **requirements** (what the business wants) early.
- Builds **shared ownership** and speeds later acceptance.

## Practice
- Iterative sessions moving from high-level bubble diagrams to detailed attribute/fact lists.
- Driven by the **[[Four-Step Dimensional Design Process]]** and anchored to the **[[Enterprise Data Warehouse Bus Matrix|bus matrix]]**.
