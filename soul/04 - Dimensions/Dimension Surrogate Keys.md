# Dimension Surrogate Keys

Every [[Dimension Tables|dimension table]] should have a **surrogate key** as its primary key: a meaningless, sequentially assigned integer generated by the warehouse, with **no business meaning**. The [[Fact Tables|fact table]] joins to dimensions **only** through these surrogate keys.

## Why not just use the source's natural key?
- **Insulation from source changes** — reused, recycled, or reformatted operational keys can't corrupt the warehouse.
- **Enables [[Slowly Changing Dimensions|SCD Type 2]]** — the *only* way to keep multiple rows ("versions") of the same entity is a surrogate key distinct from the [[Natural, Durable, and Supernatural Keys|natural key]].
- **Performance** — a single narrow integer key makes fact-table joins and indexes small and fast (vs. wide multi-column natural keys).
- **Handles "not applicable / unknown"** — special surrogate rows (e.g., key = −1) absorb nulls and missing FKs; **never** put a null in a fact-table foreign key.
- **Integrates heterogeneous sources** — one product dimension can unify keys from several systems.

## Rules
- Assign in **ETL** (a key generator / lookup), never expose to users.
- Keep the **natural/durable key** as an attribute for lineage (see [[Natural, Durable, and Supernatural Keys]]).
